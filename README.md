# My Favorite Series: *The Pitt*

![The Pitt](https://www.primevideo.com/detail/0NNDMY1ZJA2IUV77870P5X6EZ4)

> *"A 15-hour shift in the ER, one hour per episode."*

---

## Summary
***The Pitt*** is a medical drama on **HBO Max** created by **R. Scott Gemmill**. It follows the staff of the fictional *Pittsburgh Trauma Medical Center* emergency department during one long, chaotic shift. Each episode covers one hour of that shift, so the story plays out almost in real time.

- **Season 1** takes place during a single day shift that ends up involving a mass-casualty emergency.
- **Season 2** takes place over the Fourth of July weekend, one of the busiest times for an ER.

## Why I Like It
1. **It feels real.** The one-hour-per-episode format puts you inside the show. It makes me feel as if I'm doing a shift with them.
2. **The characters have depth.** Doctors and nurses are shown as realistic people dealing with stress, mistakes, and their own struggles.
3. **It shows how hard healthcare work is.** The show doesn't hide staffing, time, and resource problems.
4. **The acting is excellent.** The acting really helps set the tone of realism which helps pull you in even more into this fictional world
5. **No background music** There are no music for when somethings gets intense or for when something bad happens, making it even more grounding as the loud and chaotic ambience of the ED shine through instead.
